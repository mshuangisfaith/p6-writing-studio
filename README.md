# Ms H’s Writing Studio — Primary 5 & 6

An interactive composition revision site prepared for Ms Huang's class. British English, familiar classroom story situations and focused direct-speech practice.

## Included

- Six theme families with adaptable ideas.
- The five senses; show, don't tell; exploding a moment; turning points; characterisation.
- Eight direct-speech patterns, a sentence builder with 120 combinations, twelve multiple-choice questions, six sentence repairs and speaker-paragraph practice.
- Eight groups of saying verbs, organised by emotion or manner.
- Five complications: a physical fight, a quarrel, a dilemma, a moral dilemma and a fire.
- Five viewpoints, including an animal, adult, elderly person and younger child.
- Three flashback frames, eleven starter phrases and three ending techniques.
- Classroom conventions and a seventeen-item final checklist.

## Open or host

Open `index.html` in a modern browser, keeping the five site files together. No installation, external libraries, account or internet connection is needed after downloading the files. For GitHub Pages, place the folder within an existing publishing source or publish the files from the root of a new repository. Open the folder URL ending in `/`.

Direct-speech repair text, selected answers and checklist ticks remain in memory in the current tab. Reloading clears them. Nothing is submitted to a server. The sentence-repair checker accepts the supplied corrections and normalises straight/curly quotation marks; other grammatical rewrites may also be valid.

The optional `document.modelContext` speech-builder tool is feature-detected; ordinary use does not depend on browser support.

## Editorial basis

Adapted from the available 2026 teaching conversations: *Proofread And Format Stories*, *Create Friendship Slides*, and *Extend Figurative Sports Writing*. The teacher's current instructions govern classroom conventions. Earlier notes were consulted for phrase preferences; no pupil names, class identifiers, marks or original pupil scripts are published. Character names are the fictional names used in the teaching examples. Short examples have been adapted and newly written for these activities rather than presented as verbatim reproductions.

2025 conversations were omitted at the teacher's request. This site is a revision resource, not a prediction of an examination topic.
